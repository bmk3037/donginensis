FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you have liquid hydrogen station references?

Yes. We supplied the VFD, PLC and UPS control system for the SK E&S liquid hydrogen refueling station and continue its commercial operation pilot.

Do you hold hydrogen certifications or patents?

We are a designated hydrogen specialist company (Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy) and have two registered patents and three pending applications related to liquid hydrogen station control systems, covering power control logic, operational safety, energy efficiency optimization and equipment protection algorithms.

What do you control at a liquid hydrogen station?

We control and monitor station equipment, combining precise control of pumps and thermal oil circulation with real-time monitoring, energy efficiency and safety management in one control system.

Do you control hydrogen-powered vessels?

Yes. In the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries project on safe small hydrogen-powered vessels (2022.4–2026.12), we handle integrated control, monitoring and system integration of fuel cells, propulsion motors and hydrogen tanks.

Can you collect data from hydrogen equipment?

Yes. We are building, step by step, a structure that collects operating and power data while the panel controls the equipment, and the Intelligent Control Panel connects it to the cloud or your server.

How long does it take and what does it cost?

It depends on the equipment, safety requirements and the number and specification of panels. Send us your specification or equipment list and we will quote with a proposed configuration.
