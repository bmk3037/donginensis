FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our motors or pumps?

No. The equipment stays as it is; the control panel is replaced with an Intelligent Control Panel or newly installed. We recommend the best approach after surveying your existing panels.

Can it be installed where there is no internet?

Yes, with an LTE communication module. We check the site's communication environment during the survey.

How are data security and equipment safety handled?

The site and the cloud are connected through TLS encryption. Interlocks and final control stay with the on-site PLC; the server never controls equipment directly.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number and size of the equipment and the data points. We provide a configuration and quotation after the site survey.
