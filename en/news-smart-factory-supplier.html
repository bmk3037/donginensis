DONG-IN ENSIS registers as a smart factory supplier and unveils its smart manufacturing data solution

  • — Supplier competency assessment applied for; preparing for Data Voucher and AI Voucher supplier registration, M.AX specialist company designation and Innovative Product designation
2026.10.05DONG-IN ENSIS

DONG-IN ENSIS, a smart manufacturing solution company, has registered as a supplier for Korea's smart factory support program and unveiled a smart manufacturing data solution for collecting, standardizing and monitoring industrial OT data and connecting it to AI. Following its registration, the company has also applied for the smart factory supplier competency assessment. The solution collects operating data from PLCs, VFDs, EOCRs, sensors and meters on the shop floor in real time, then cleans and standardizes it into manufacturing data ready for equipment monitoring, AI anomaly detection, digital twins and MES/ERP integration.

Highlights

  • Control → Data → Intelligence — Not just a monitoring system: the solution links equipment control, data and AI in one structure, turning equipment data into standardized manufacturing data assets.
  • Five-level architecture — Field/Control, OT Connectivity, Energient Industrial DataHub, a web-based PWA dashboard, and AI/Digital Twin. Major industrial protocols are supported, including Modbus TCP/RTU, MQTT and OPC UA.
  • New and existing equipment — New equipment sends data straight from the PLC; existing equipment connects to the same data infrastructure through a gateway.
  • Packages — Basic for companies adopting a smart factory for the first time, Standard for upgrades (data standardization, DataHub, API, MES/ERP integration), and AI Advanced for manufacturers using AI (anomaly detection, condition monitoring, digital twin integration).
  • Supplier competency assessment — Following its smart factory supplier registration, the company has applied for the supplier competency assessment. Based on the result, it plans to apply for designation as a Smart Manufacturing Technology Specialist Company by the Ministry of SMEs and Startups.
  • Vouchers, M.AX and Innovative Product — The company is preparing to register as a Data Voucher supplier with its Industrial OT Data Engineering Service, which collects, processes, standardizes and analyzes equipment operating data. As mid- to long-term goals, it is also preparing for AI Voucher supplier registration, M.AX specialist company designation by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy, and Innovative Product designation by the Public Procurement Service.

Building on OT engineering experience in designing and building PLC, VFD and control panel systems, DONG-IN ENSIS can deliver control engineering and data engineering as a single supplier. It brings field experience from shipbuilding and marine, hydrogen and energy, industrial machinery and plant control to the manufacturing floor.

"We go beyond simply controlling equipment on the shop floor: we collect and standardize equipment data and connect it to manufacturing data that AI can use," the company said.
