DONG-IN ENSIS succeeds in installing a hybrid system on the eco-friendly Chungnam hospital ship

  • — Commendation for building eco-friendly vessels at the Chungnam hospital ship commissioning ceremony
2023.08.30DONG-IN ENSIS

The "Chungnam hospital ship", which uses technology from DONG-IN ENSIS, a supplier of eco-friendly hybrid propulsion systems, has entered service. Following the Gyeongnam Cheongjeong-ho commissioned in April, the electric propulsion enabled by DONG-IN ENSIS technology has greatly reduced CO2 emissions compared with conventional diesel vessels. At the commissioning ceremony at Daecheon Port in Boryeong, the company's then-CEO Baek Nam-soon received a commendation for building eco-friendly vessels.

Highlights

  • Operation — Considering shallow waters around islands, the vessel uses hybrid electric propulsion, running on the electric motor at low speed and over short distances.
  • Scope of supply — The company supplied the propulsion system and integrated control system — the core of the transition to eco-friendly vessels — contributing to the hybrid conversion of Korean vessels.
  • Onshore business — The company is also expanding into onshore eco-friendly equipment, including winning control work for a liquid hydrogen station.
