DONG-IN ENSIS finally selected as a "Strong Small Business" by the Ministry of Employment and Labor

  • — Following its selection as a Busan Strategic Industry Leader and Legend 50+ company
2024.05.24DONG-IN ENSIS

DONG-IN ENSIS has been finally selected as a Strong Small Business by the Ministry of Employment and Labor. Strong Small Businesses are selected through a comprehensive evaluation of wage arrears, industrial accidents, credit rating and number of insured employees, identifying companies with innovation and growth potential.

Highlights

  • Government support — Benefits include hiring support, corporate promotion, preference in various selections, financial and lending preferences, and tax audit preferences.
  • Business areas — The company provides customized engineering solutions that raise the efficiency of eco-friendly vessels and hydrogen refueling station equipment.
  • This year's achievements — The selection follows Busan's Strategic Industry Leader and the regional specialization project Legend 50+.
