DONG-IN ENSIS takes a new leap as a leader in hydrogen energy engineering solutions

  • — Supplying the core control system for a liquid hydrogen station project led by a major Korean company
2024.06.05DONG-IN ENSIS

DONG-IN ENSIS is participating as the core control system supplier in a liquid hydrogen refueling station project led by a major Korean company. A liquid hydrogen station stores and dispenses hydrogen in liquid form, offering higher storage and transport efficiency and faster refueling than conventional stations.

Highlights

  • Integrated control system — The company supplies a control system that integrates real-time monitoring, energy-efficiency optimization and safety management, supporting stable and efficient station operation.
  • Minimizing downtime — Drawing on long experience in system control, it prevents problems in advance and reduces equipment downtime to raise operating efficiency.

"This order is the result of our technology and reliability being recognized," a company official said. "We are pleased to contribute to revitalizing Korea's hydrogen economy."
