FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of control panels do you build?

Automation and PLC panels, drive and motor control panels, control consoles and system panels for marine and plant use, and the Intelligent Control Panel that also collects equipment data.

Where are the panels built?

In our own Yangsan Plants 1 and 2 (completed 2023) in Gyeongsangnam-do and our Gijang Plant in Busan. The head office and R&D center are in Busanjin-gu, Busan.

What do you test before shipment?

The factory acceptance test (FAT) covers 13 items including appearance and nameplate, enclosure and IP rating, clearance and creepage, mechanical operation, protective device settings, insulation resistance, dielectric withstand, protective earth, power and phase sequence, I/O and communication, interlocks and emergency stop, drives and PLC, alarms and protection, and every I/O point is checked separately. Results are recorded in a test report, and customers can witness the test.

Can you replace aging control panels?

Yes. We review the existing panel and operating method on site, design and build the replacement, and schedule the changeover around your production plan. You can also start collecting equipment data with the Intelligent Control Panel at the same time.

Can you also write the PLC program?

Yes. Panel building, PLC and drive programming, factory testing and site commissioning are done by one company. Panel and program are tested together before shipment, which shortens commissioning on site.

Which component brands do you use?

We are an official Schneider Electric SI partner and have used SIEMENS, ABB, Fuji Electric, Phoenix Contact, Danfoss, Eaton and others. We follow your specification or designated brands.

How long does it take and what does it cost?

It depends on the number of panels, ratings and specification. In our standard Intelligent Control Panel schedule, panel building takes about 5 weeks. We quote with a proposed configuration after a site review.
