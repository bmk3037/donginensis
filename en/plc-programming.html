FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you build the PLC control panel and do the PLC programming together?

Yes. DONG-IN ENSIS designs and builds PLC control panels and handles PLC programming, factory testing and site commissioning in one company. Panel and program are tested together before shipment, which shortens commissioning on site.

Which PLCs do you work with?

We are an official Schneider Electric SI partner and have used SIEMENS, ABB, Fuji Electric and other brands in our panels. Tell us the PLC model and the state of the current program, and we will confirm whether we can take it on.

Do we need a new program when replacing an existing panel?

During the site review we check the existing panel and operating method, then propose which parts to keep and which to reorganize.

Can you also collect equipment data after programming?

Yes. With the Intelligent Control Panel, the PLC controls the equipment and sends operating data over MQTT to the cloud or your server, with no extra gateway.

How do factory testing and commissioning work?

Panels and programs are tested in our factory for operation, interlocks and alarms before shipment, followed by site commissioning and operator training.

How long does it take and what does it cost?

It depends on the number of machines, I/O size, control complexity and whether a panel is built. We quote with a proposed configuration after the site review. A standard Intelligent Control Panel project takes 9–15 weeks.
