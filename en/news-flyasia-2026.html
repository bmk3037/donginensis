DONG-IN ENSIS to exhibit at FLY ASIA 2026 under the "Marine & AI" theme

  • — Presenting data infrastructure for land and marine equipment with the Intelligent Control Panel
2026.09.28DONG-IN ENSIS

DONG-IN ENSIS (CEO Baek Min-ki), a smart manufacturing solution company, will present its Intelligent Control Panel at FLY ASIA 2026, Asia's startup expo held at BEXCO Exhibition Hall 1 in Busan from October 7 to 8. Hosted by Busan Metropolitan City and organized by the Busan Technology Startup Investment Agency, this year's event focuses on the convergence of marine and artificial intelligence (AI) technologies under the theme "Blue Ocean Connect".

Highlights

  • Intelligent Control Panel — The control panel that runs the equipment also collects operating data and connects to the cloud over MQTT/TLS encryption without separate collection hardware. It records motor and pump temperature, current and vibration 24 hours a day and raises an alarm when values exceed thresholds.
  • Expanding to marine sites — Building on experience with ballast water treatment systems, electric propulsion for eco-friendly vessels and integrated control of hydrogen-powered vessel systems, the company is applying the Intelligent Control Panel to marine equipment such as LNG carrier engine rooms and cranes on offshore wind installation vessels.
  • AI anomaly detection — AI anomaly detection based on the collected equipment data is being refined at the proof-of-concept (PoC) stage.
  • Proposals by audience — For companies operating manufacturing and marine equipment, the company offers a site applicability assessment; for smart factory, AI and MES solution companies, it proposes collaboration through standardized data and APIs.

"DONG-IN ENSIS creates the flow from control to data, and from data to intelligence," said CEO Baek Min-ki. "At FLY ASIA we will share first-hand how to build equipment data infrastructure for the marine industry."
