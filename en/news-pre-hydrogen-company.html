DONG-IN ENSIS leads localization of liquid hydrogen power control, selected as a "Preliminary Hydrogen Specialized Company"

  • — Selected as a Preliminary Hydrogen Specialized Company jointly organized by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy and Busan Metropolitan City
2025.06.18DONG-IN ENSIS

DONG-IN ENSIS has been selected as a "Preliminary Hydrogen Specialized Company" jointly organized by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy and Busan Metropolitan City. The company's design and engineering capabilities in hydrogen refueling station equipment and hydrogen energy solutions for eco-friendly vessels were recognized for both technology and business potential, and it will receive Busan's support for commercialization, market development and talent development.

Highlights

  • Localizing liquid hydrogen power control — With an intelligent power control system for liquid hydrogen stations as its core project, the company is commercializing real-time precision control of pumps and thermal-oil circulation equipment, energy-saving algorithms and technologies that strengthen equipment stability.
  • Proven at a commercial site — The company has secured a demonstration base by supplying and operating its control system at an actual commercial liquid hydrogen station.
  • Marine hydrogen mobility — Participating in a Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries demonstration project, the company is securing predictive power control, output distribution and condition-based control technologies for fuel-cell-based electric propulsion of vessels.
  • Mid- to long-term strategy — The company aims to reduce dependence on imported control equipment and is pursuing the domestic hydrogen infrastructure procurement market, exports to the Middle East and Southeast Asia, and a standardization roadmap.

"We are growing into a total solution company that leads localization and technological self-reliance in hydrogen-based power infrastructure," a company official said. "Aiming to become a full Hydrogen Specialized Company, we will focus on advancing core technologies such as control algorithms, power protection circuits and smart control panels."
