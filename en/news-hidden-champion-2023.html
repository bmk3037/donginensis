DONG-IN ENSIS selected as a "Busan-type Hidden Champion and Service Small Giant"

  • — Recognized for independently developing a marine hybrid propulsion system
2023.08.29DONG-IN ENSIS

DONG-IN ENSIS has been selected as a "Busan-type Hidden Champion" and a "Service Small Giant" by Busan Metropolitan City. The selection was prompted by the company's independently developed marine hybrid propulsion system, which was installed on Korean government vessels and successfully commissioned.

Highlights

  • Busan-type Hidden Champion — A certification for promising mid-sized and small companies in Busan, providing up to KRW 150 million in support for R&D, growth strategy consulting, overseas marketing and prototype production.
  • Service Small Giant — A Busan Metropolitan City program that systematically fosters companies with competitiveness and growth potential in the service sector.
  • Technology base — With technology built in industrial and electrical control, the company is leading the localization of eco-friendly vessels.

"This selection reconfirms the company's growth potential," a company official said. "We will contribute to the eco-friendly transition of Korean vessels."
