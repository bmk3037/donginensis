DONG-IN ENSIS selected as a 2027 Busan Strategic Industry Leading Company

  • — Selected in the convergence parts and materials sector; certified for three years, 2027–2029
  • — Intelligent Control Panel combines equipment control and data acquisition to support digital transformation in manufacturing and marine industries
2026.11.14DONG-IN ENSIS · Press release

DONG-IN ENSIS CO., LTD. has been selected as a 2027 Busan Strategic Industry Leading Company in the convergence parts and materials sector.

According to the selection notice from Busan Technopark, DONG-IN ENSIS was selected after review by the final selection committee on November 5, 2026. The certification runs for three years, from 2027 to 2029.

The Busan Strategic Industry Leading Company program identifies and develops competitive companies that will drive the regional economy. Busan Metropolitan City and Busan Technopark evaluate companies in nine strategic industries on economic performance, growth and innovation, together with technological competitiveness, export orientation and the ability to deliver results. The convergence parts and materials sector, in which DONG-IN ENSIS was selected, covers smart manufacturing, robotics and advanced machinery.

35 years of industrial control experience

Founded in 1991, DONG-IN ENSIS is an industrial control specialist that handles everything from the design and manufacture of automatic control panels, PLC control panels and inverter control panels to control program development, on-site installation and commissioning. It has built control systems across manufacturing, shipbuilding, marine and hydrogen energy sites.

An Intelligent Control Panel that combines control and data acquisition

Its flagship solution, the Intelligent Control Panel, combines equipment control with operating data acquisition. While the on-site PLC keeps the control logic and safety interlocks, the panel collects equipment data such as temperature, current, vibration and energy consumption and connects it to the cloud or the customer's servers. The collected data is organised in a standard data structure and exposed through APIs so that it can be linked to manufacturing execution systems (MES), AI analytics and digital twins.

Building on its operational technology (OT) capability for control panels and field equipment, DONG-IN ENSIS also offers a business model of working with smart factory, AI and MES solution companies. DONG-IN ENSIS takes care of the field side — control panel manufacturing, PLC and sensor integration, data acquisition and server connection — so that IT companies can focus on their platforms and analytics services.

The company is currently building equipment data infrastructure and running a proof of concept (PoC) for AI anomaly detection, and has set out a roadmap toward anomaly detection and predictive maintenance based on real operating data and integration with digital twins.

The certificate ceremony for the 2027 Busan Strategic Industry Leading Companies is scheduled for December 3, 2026, at the Grand Moment Classic Hall in Nam-gu, Busan.
